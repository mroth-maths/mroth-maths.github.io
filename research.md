Representation theory is a tool to learn more about different algebraic objects using linear algebra over a field. Often it comes in two flavours, when the field has characteristic zero (e.g. the complex numbers) and positive characteristic, the latter being much less understood than the latter. One question I like to consider is whether one can and how to bridge these two aspects. 

During my Phd, the objects I considered were finite groups of Lie type. The bridge between their complex and positive representation is given by a decomposition matrix, which is often unitriangular. 

At the moment, I am looking at 2-categories, where the main example are diagrammatic Hecke categories, a generalisation of Soergel bimodules.
